About Build to Wear

Build to Wear started with a simple question:

"Why do portfolios only live on screens?"

As a designer and builder, I wanted something more personal—something that didn't just sit in a browser tab or get lost in a Google Drive folder. I wanted to wear my work. I wanted it to spark conversations. To remind me what I stand for. To help others understand me at a glance.

That's how Build to Wear began.

We create custom-made hoodies that showcase your work, story, and creative identity. It's part portfolio, part uniform, part reminder of what you're building and why.

Each hoodie is designed with you—from layout to typography, from color to message. Whether you're a founder, designer, artist, or engineer, we help you translate your process, values, and voice into a wearable piece you'll actually live in.

It's not merch.

It's not print-on-demand fluff.

It's a physical extension of your creative self.

We only release a few hoodie slots at a time so we can go deep, not wide. Every hoodie is a collaboration. Every stitch says something.

Because your work deserves to be seen.

And sometimes, a hoodie can say more than a slide deck ever could.
